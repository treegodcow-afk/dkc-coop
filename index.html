Donkey Kong Country Co-op

Load your own dump of Donkey Kong Country (USA) (Rev 1) for the SNES. The page checks it, applies the co-op patch, and runs it with snes9x. The game file never leaves your browser.

You're joining a friend's game as player 2. Their game streams to you here and your controls run the second Kong. Press Start (Enter) to join once both Kongs are in a level.
